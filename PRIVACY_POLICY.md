# Privacy Policy for DeTube

**Last Updated:** May 2026

DeTube is an open-source browser extension developed by [GeekStash.dev](https://geekstash.dev) designed to provide a distraction-free YouTube experience. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle your information and protect your privacy.

## 1. Information Collection

**We do not collect any personal information.** You can use the DeTube extension without providing your name, email address, or any other personal data. 

## 2. Data Sharing and Third Parties

Since we do not collect any personal data, **we do not sell, rent, trade, or share any of your data with third parties.** Your privacy is fully respected.

## 3. Extension Permissions & Local Data

DeTube operates entirely on your local device. The extension requires certain browser permissions (such as `storage`, `scripting`, and `tabs`) to function properly. These are used strictly to:
- Save your custom interface preferences locally in your browser.
- Apply CSS modifications directly to your active YouTube tabs to hide specific elements.
- Detect YouTube navigation to re-apply your settings seamlessly without page reloads.

**We do not track, collect, or transmit your browsing history, YouTube usage, or any other activity data to our servers or any external services.**

## 4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if we introduce new features (such as an optional newsletter). Any changes will be clearly reflected in this document.
